The 5 Essential Website Features

Each section is designed with a specific conversion goal in mind.

01
❤️

Donation Section

The heart of every NGO website. Offer preset donation amounts (₹100, ₹500, ₹1000, custom), a clear progress bar toward a fundraising goal, and a secure one-click payment flow. Add recurring donation options and tax-exemption (80G) reminders to maximise conversion.

Conversion critical
03
📊

Impact Showcase

Numbers that prove your work is real. Animated counters for lives touched, meals served, trees planted, or scholarships funded turn abstract mission into tangible proof.

Trust builder
🎓 Scholarships funded
🌱 Trees planted
🍱 Meals distributed
👨‍👩‍👧 Families reached
02
🤝

Volunteer Registration

A simple form asking for name, skills, availability, and city. Makes it effortless to join. Show how many volunteers have already signed up to trigger social proof.

Community growth
04
💬

Testimonials

Quotes and short videos from beneficiaries, donors, and volunteers. Authentic stories overcome doubt better than any statistic. Include photo, name, and location for credibility.

Social proof
05
📣

Active Campaigns

Highlight ongoing drives — education kits, flood relief, tree plantation. Show a progress bar, deadline, and a clear "Support This" CTA to create urgency and boost engagement.

Urgency driver

Feature Notes

Why each feature matters

01
Donation Section
Preset amounts reduce decision fatigue. Progress bars toward a goal create urgency. Recurring options grow long-term revenue. 80G badge builds trust.
↑ Drives direct revenue
02
Volunteer Form
Short form (name, skill, city, availability). Show volunteer count for social proof. Auto-email confirmation keeps momentum alive after sign-up.
↑ Grows community
03
Impact Showcase
Animated counters make abstract numbers feel real. Before/after photos from programs. Downloadable annual impact report for corporate donors.
↑ Builds credibility
04
Testimonials
Photos + first-name + location = authenticity. Video testimonials outperform text 3×. Rotate donor, volunteer, and beneficiary voices for diversity.
↑ Overcomes scepticism
05
Active Campaigns
Show goal vs raised with a progress bar. Add a deadline for urgency. Each campaign page should have a unique URL for easy sharing on social media.
↑ Creates urgency
